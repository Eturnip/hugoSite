<p class="c6">Title: Battle Wagon</p>

<p class="c4"></p>

<p class="c6">Author: Owen McManus</p>

<p class="c4"></p>

<p class="c6">Draft date: 04/01/2019</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">INT. SMOKEY ROOM. A ROUND TABLE SITS UNDER THE SINGLE POOL OF LIGHT IN THE ROOM. A PILE OF MAPS AND BLUEPRINTS LAY STREWN IN THE CENTER. THERE IS A GROUP OF GRIM LOOKING MEN AND WOMEN POSITIONED AROUND THE TABLE ON WOOD AND METAL STACKING CHAIRS.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">You know why I called you here. Ya'll are the meanest bunch of miscreants that I have ever had the misfortune to come across, and it pains me to say that this, but your country needs you.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">Again.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Billson chomps a half finished cigar and tips the chair back .</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Yes Billson. Again. Now I'm not going to beat around the bush. This mission is dangerous. Some of you might not be coming home. There won't be any state funerals for those who don't, and there won't be any medals for those that do.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">CARLILE</p>

<p class="c2">We already ghosts, ain't we. Don't bother me none.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Nodding and grunts of acknowledgement from around the table.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Okay, okay. Before we get to the briefing, I do have one issue I would like addressed.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">Sarge, you're really going to question me and my team. After Tokyo, after Berlin, after Montreal?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Now I know that your team has a solid track record with these kinds of Ops. I know that you will do what needs doing to get the job done. That's not the problem. I just have one question.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c6 c7">(clearly annoyed)</p>

<p class="c2">Spit it out.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Where is the battle wagon.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Team shoot confused looks at each other.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Billson raises a hand to settle the team down.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">The what?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">The battle wagon.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Team continues to look confused. They have no idea what the sergeant is talking about.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">In Zaragosa. The van you welded armor panels and machineguns to and used to break into the arms dealers fortress.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c2">Oh, the Steel Zephyr. He's talking about the Steel Zephyr. Yeah. Had to ditch it in the Ebro.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Okay, so why is it in the river?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">CARLILE</p>

<p class="c2">Had tae douse it to deactivate the Super C-4 strapped to the bottom, didn't we.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">TIMMINS</p>

<p class="c6 c7">(points a mocking thumb at Sarge)</p>

<p class="c2">Sounds like he never had to drown some Super C-4. I suppose that's why you hire the experts.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Alright. Fair enough. Bottom of the Ebro. So where's the one you built out of a train engine in Dhaka.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c2">The Light Rail Ravager! Oh, I miss her. Really packed a punch and extra roof space for the missile racks.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">And where is it? In the river?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">Lake.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Sarge shakes his head for a moment.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Do you folks see my problem.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">Not really Sarge. Speak plain and we'll see what we can do for you.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">How many missions did you run for us last year?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">Timmins?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">TIMMINS</p>

<p class="c2">17 Boss. If you count the Federico twins as 2.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Sure. 17. And how many battle wagons did you build?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Timmins considers for a moment.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">TIMMINS</p>

<p class="c2">Including the Steel Zephyr?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Yes. All the battle wagons.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c2">Can we stop calling them battle wagons. They got names.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Fine. How many.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">TIMMINS</p>

<p class="c4"></p>

<p class="c3">18</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">You didn't even run that many missions.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c2">Built two in Daegu. Carlile had his own.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">CARLILE</p>

<p class="c2">Only 'cause Timmins said I had the Kimchi farts and I didnea.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">You built a battle-</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">Zhang pulls a sour look.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Okay, okay. I don't care why. Do any of you know what one of those monstrosities costs?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">CARLILE</p>

<p class="c2">Well we don't buy 'em. We build 'em, don't we.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">I buy them. I pay for them. Every time I hire you for a mission I pay the expenses. Armor plating, cannon shells, caltrops, arc welders. I have to bury all your battle wagons in secret budgets and military slush funds. By the way, why do you need a new arc welder every time? Why don't you use the old one?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">I think we are all missing the bigger picture here. We ran 18 Ops-</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">TIMMINS</p>

<p class="c4"></p>

<p class="c3">17.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">17 Ops. Every single one of them a resounding success.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">In Tampa you drove a Russian fighter jet through a mall.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">TIMMINS</p>

<p class="c2">You sent us that jet.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Yes. Because I thought you were going to fly it. You turned it into a tank!</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c2">We saved the world from Moloch and his global terrorists.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Where is it now?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">TIMMINS</p>

<p class="c2">The mall?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">The battle wagon! Where is it now.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c2">The Suffering Butterfly.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">What?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c2">That's her name. The Suffering Butterfly.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">I don't care! Where is it?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">ZHANG</p>

<p class="c2">In the bay. I couldn't let Moloch get her.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Could you have used the battle wagon from Tokyo to stop Moloch?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c6">The team is confused again.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">CARLILE</p>

<p class="c2">She's in the bay, isn't she.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">If she wasn't in the bay. Imagine just once that you reused a battle wagon. Again. On the next mission. Could you have used that battle wagon to stop Moloch.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c6 c7">(bewildered by the idea of it)</p>

<p class="c2">I mean, it's possible, but how would we even have gotten it out of the water?</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">It was a hovercraft! I don't even know how you sank it.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">CARLILE</p>

<p class="c2">Weren't easy. We're very good at our jobs.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">SARGE</p>

<p class="c2">Alright, alright. The current mission is time critical. We only have 24 hours to get you in and get those hostages out. We can talk about this more when you get back.</p>

<p class="c6 c7">(Sarge shuffles through some maps and pulls one to the top)</p>

<p class="c2">We have possible entry and extraction points here and here. Known tangos are here, here, and here.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>

<p class="c3">BILLSON</p>

<p class="c6 c7">(leaning back in the chair again)</p>

<p class="c2">No need Sarge. Me and the team already worked out the plan on the way over here. All we need from you is an attack helicopter and the biggest set of tractor tires you can find.</p>

<p class="c0"></p>
