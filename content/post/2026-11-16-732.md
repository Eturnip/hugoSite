Best Games - Star Wars : Knights of the Old Republic

I never played many RPGs growing up. I dabbled with the SSI gold box D&D games. I played a little of Westwood’s Eye of the Beholder. I never really played an Ultima or Wizardry. I didn’t spend more than a few hours with a Final Fantasy game until well into the Playstation 2 era. I did finish Chrono Trigger when you could emulate it in 1999. The only game that I finished prior to that, and is similar to an RPG, was Star Control 2, and that was back in the early 90s. I used to think that maybe RPGs just weren’t my thing.

Sometime around 2004-2005 I had an Xbox and people sure were talking up this Star Wars game. I liked Star Wars, I liked games, seemed like I should try out this Star Wars game. It turned out that I picked a hell of a good time to get into RPGs.

If you are just getting into RPGs, Star Wars : Knights of the Old Republic is a great place to start. It does away with a lot of the slower, more fiddly, elements that tend to plague RPGs. There are all the stats, equipment, assignable points, and turn based combat that you want out of an RPG, but it's also a realtime action game.

Bioware's previous Dungeons and Dragons RPG games had a similar system of realtime movement and combat, but you could pause at any time to act more strategically. Those games were very much attempting to emulate the table-top RPG experience and didn't particularly lean into the idea of being action games first. Kotor, very effectively, hybridized the genre.

If I had to guess, I would say that there are probably a large number of players who never paused when playing Kotor. There were probably a few who didn't even realize that you could. Queueing commands and attacks is so snappy and intuitive that you could get away with playing mostly as an action game.

I know that there were a few action style RPGs prior to Kotor, but I never played them. As far as I knew at the time, this was a first. And that move to a more action oriented game was all it took to make a convert of me. It seems like it worked for a lot of other people too.

Of course, an evolution of the combat system is not enough to make an RPG a classic. It also has to hit on all the other important RPG elements. Does it have good writing? Check. Are the characters interesting? Check. Is the overarching story engaging? Are the quests and small stories worth your time? Check and Check.

Kotor also has one final hurdle to clear. Is it a good Star Wars game? Kotor is not only a good Star Wars game, but at the time it came out it was one of the best Star Wars stories going. We were smack dab in the middle of the prequel trilogy and appetites for Star Wars stories that didn't involve a Skywalker were probably at an all time high. This was a depiction of a Star Wars Galaxy that was recognizable, but far in the past. It stands among some of the best Star Wars storytelling.

I know that there has been a remake in development, so if that ever comes out that would probably be the way to play this game, but as it stands I think the game is modern enough that it probably still holds up today. It is, after all, one of the Best Games.
